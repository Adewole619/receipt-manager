# Receipt Manager Requirements

## What Problem Does the App Solve?

Many people collect paper receipts or digital receipts from different places and struggle to keep track of their purchases. Receipts can be lost, damaged, or become difficult to find when needed for budgeting, returns, warranties, or expense tracking.

Receipt Manager solves this problem by providing a simple way to store, organize, and retrieve receipts digitally from an Android device.

## Who Will Use It?

Receipt Manager is designed for:

- Individuals who want to keep track of personal purchases
- People who need receipts for returns or warranty purposes
- Small business owners who need a simple receipt tracking solution
- Anyone who wants a digital alternative to storing paper receipts

## What Can Users Do?

In Version 1, users can:

- Create and save receipts
- Add receipt details such as merchant name, date, amount, and category
- View a list of saved receipts
- Search for receipts
- Edit existing receipt information
- Delete receipts
- Attach receipt images
- Store receipt data locally on their device

## What Is Not Included in Version 1?

The following features are not included in Version 1:

- User accounts and authentication
- Cloud storage and synchronization
- Multi-device access
- Automatic receipt scanning using OCR
- Bank account integration
- Payment tracking
- Advanced financial reports
- Sharing receipts with other users
- Online backup services

These features may be considered for future versions.

## What Does Success Look Like?

The project will be considered successful when users can:

- Easily save and organize their receipts
- Quickly find receipts when needed
- Maintain a digital record of their purchases
- Use the application without requiring technical knowledge
- Reliably store receipt information on their Android device

Version 1 success will also include:

- A working Android application
- Stable local data storage
- A simple and intuitive user interface
- Reliable receipt management functionality
